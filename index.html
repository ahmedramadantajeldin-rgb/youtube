Shorts Filtering Status & Solutions

✓ Active in PWA
Shorts Playback & Navigation Intercepted: Direct Shorts playback is blocked. Incoming or pasted Shorts links are intercepted, giving you options to convert to standard YouTube desktop/mobile player (watch?v=) or return to the regular feed.
✓ Active in PWA
Bypass Algorithmic Shorts Shelves: Set the default launch destination to Subscriptions to bypass YouTube Home's algorithmic Shorts carousel completely upon app launch.
ℹ Platform Boundary
In-Page Feed & Search Shelves on youtube.com: Because this PWA navigates to the official YouTube website (cross-origin), web platform security (Same-Origin Policy) strictly prevents external web apps from altering YouTube's internal DOM elements, recommendations, or search result shelves once you are browsing on youtube.com.
How to completely block Shorts shelves inside youtube.com

To eliminate Shorts carousels and shelves from YouTube's Home feed, recommendations, and search results on your device:

  1. Browser Content Blocker (uBlock Origin / Adblock): Add these cosmetic filter rules to hide Shorts elements across YouTube: 
    ! Hide YouTube Shorts shelves on Home and Search
youtube.com##ytd-rich-shelf-renderer[is-shorts]
youtube.com##ytd-reel-shelf-renderer
youtube.com##ytm-reel-shelf-renderer

! Hide Shorts navigation buttons and links
youtube.com##ytd-guide-entry-renderer:has(a[title="Shorts"])
youtube.com##ytd-mini-guide-entry-renderer[aria-label="Shorts"]
youtube.com##a[href^="/shorts"]

! Hide Shorts in search results & recommendations
youtube.com##ytd-video-renderer:has(a[href*="/shorts/"])
youtube.com##ytd-compact-video-renderer:has(a[href*="/shorts/"])
  2. Browser Extensions (Desktop & Mobile): Install extensions such as Unhook or Hide YouTube Shorts in Chrome, Firefox, Kiwi Browser (Android), or Orion (iOS).
  3. Native YouTube Option: On any Shorts shelf on youtube.com, tap the three dots (⋮) and select Show fewer Shorts to suppress the shelf for 30 days.

Unofficial shortcut. Videos, accounts and everything else are provided by youtube.com. Depending on your device, YouTube opens in an in-app window or the YouTube app.
